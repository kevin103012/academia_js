# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web

## Stack

React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and Lucide.

## Users

- Students explore courses, practice, and track their learning.
- Teachers organize courses and class materials.
- Academic coordinators review progress and follow-up alerts.
- Administrators manage the demo users and catalog.

## Product Purpose

Junior’s Sparks is a local demonstration of the educational workflows described in the two reference PDFs in `docs/`. The public site gives visitors a route into the role-specific campus.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Demo sign-in and content use browser-local storage. The educational records are illustrative. The study tutor uses prepared local responses. The supplied logo and the 60/30/10 ivory, piano black and surf turquoise palette define the brand. The UI follows the Spanish terminology in the reference documents.
